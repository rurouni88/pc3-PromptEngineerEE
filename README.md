## 🎮 At a Glance

- **Deploy-phase skill gameplay** — Glitches erupt in the live deploy stream; tap them before their fuses burn out. Bosses, cascades, corruption, meltdowns
- **Prompt quality you can read** — Tokens carry tags; tickets carry needs. Match them for fewer glitches, stack hype for bigger hotfix payouts
- **LTTC + a fake DORA dashboard** — Lead Time to Change banded Elite/Good/Poor. The Cobra Effect is a feature: chase Elite and ship garbage faster
- **Career ladder & achievements** — Junior → CTO, tier-gated tokens and tickets, 16 puns with predicates
- **Modular content profiles** — Engineering Mode now, Architect DLC gated (42 requirements, you meet 38)
- **Seeded runs** — Deterministic, replayable, shareable (same seed + same taps = same deploy)
- **Satirical validation** — Every error log, Teams DM, and management notification is a parody of real industry pain
- **Mobile-first** — Thumb-zone UI, touch targets ≥ 44px, h-dvh viewport
